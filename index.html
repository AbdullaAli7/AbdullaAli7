About Me

Technology has been a constant since childhood — navigating operating systems before fully understanding what they were, fixing family computers, and spending hours learning how software actually worked underneath. That curiosity never left; it evolved into a CS degree at The George Washington University, published research, and a drive to build systems that are technically rigorous and practically useful.

Professionally, I've worked as a Senior Software Engineer in AI Evaluation at Alignerr — designing multi-turn prompts for open-source repositories, evaluating production LLM outputs against real codebases, steering model behavior through iterative PR-style review, and contributing to training data pipelines that encode production engineering standards. The work demands the same investigative precision as security analysis: verify the claim, trace it to the code, document the finding.

On the research side, I've published at HCII 2026 and KDD 2026, building ML pipelines with XGBoost, SHAP explainability, and benchmark evaluation methodology from the ground up. On the product side, I ship full-stack applications — sports analytics platforms, AI chatbots, penalty kick scouting tools — end to end.

My interest in IT and infrastructure runs parallel to the ML work. I'm drawn to the systems layer: how networks are secured, how endpoints are managed, how enterprise technology is kept reliable. I hold the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate and am always looking to deepen that side of my practice. Multilingual in English, Arabic, Kurdish, and Turkish. Former founder of Fade Nine LLC.
